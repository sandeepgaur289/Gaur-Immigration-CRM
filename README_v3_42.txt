GAUR CRM v3.42 — ENROLLMENT PERFORMANCE + MD CASH BOOK

1) BEST EMPLOYEE / AM PERFORMANCE
• Ranking is based ONLY on actual enrollments from Client Cases.
• Lead allocation, data quality, hot/warm leads, conversion, payment, revenue, collection and manager rating do NOT affect Best Employee.
• Highest enrollment count = Best Employee.
• Enrollment Score is enrollment-only.

2) PAYMENT PRIVACY
• Payment / collection / revenue is MD confidential.
• MD sees Enrollments + Revenue on the live top bar.
• GM / AM / Reception / other portals see Enrollment count only.
• Non-MD competition API responses have revenue removed.
• Payment popup amount is hidden for non-MD.
• Finance, Bank Manager, Payment Accounts, Receipts and Cash Book are MD-only.
• GM can create/view enrollment cases but does not see or enter payment fields.
• Payment updates are MD-only.

3) DAILY CASH BOOK + MONTHLY ACCOUNTS
• New MD-only screen: /finance/cash-book
• Based on the supplied Excel sample: Inflow on left, Outflow on right.
• By / Particular / Cash or Bank / Amount.
• Daily Inflow, Daily Outflow and Net Balance.
• Monthly Receipt, Monthly Expenses/Refunds and Net Cash Flow.
• Monthly transaction register includes date, voucher, company, mode/bank, reference, amount, entered by and exact entry timestamp.
• Print / Save PDF + Excel export.

4) Existing structured payment ledger, enrollment celebration, birthday celebration and voice features remain.
