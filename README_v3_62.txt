GAUR CRM v3.62 — BANK MANAGER ADD / DELETE

• GM can add Bank/Cash accounts for her own company.
• MD can add accounts for Smart Choice or White Wave.
• Bank Manager now shows a visible Add Bank / Cash Account form.
• Each active account has a Remove Account button.
• Remove is a SAFE SOFT DELETE:
  - account disappears from active Bank Manager,
  - old transactions and reports are NOT deleted,
  - audit log records who removed it.
• GM can remove only accounts belonging to her own company.
• MD can manage both companies.
• Account number remains masked for GM; MD sees full number.
• Existing v3.61 daily/weekly/monthly/yearly reports remain.
