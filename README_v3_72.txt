GAUR CRM v3.72 — Enrollment Delete + Recycle Bin

Access:
• MD + GM can move an enrollment record to Recycle Bin.
• GM can delete only her own company's enrollment.
• Only MD can open Enrollment Recycle Bin.
• Only MD can Restore.
• Only MD can permanently Final Delete.

Soft delete:
• Enrollment disappears from active Enrollment Report and Payment Completion.
• It is excluded from client-case revenue/enrollment calculations.
• Payment details and screenshots remain preserved in Recycle Bin.
• Original Lead is not deleted.

Final delete:
• Only possible from MD Recycle Bin.
• Removes enrollment record and linked payment proofs.
• Removes linked competition enrollment event.
• Audit log remains.
